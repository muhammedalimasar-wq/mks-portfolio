OUR PROCESS

We design… we detail… we build

Most delay and rework starts at the drawing stage, not on site, so that is where we put our discipline: we build it on screen first.

01

Review the tender

Constructability and BOQ review of the drawings and specifications, so scope is clear before work begins.

02

Coordinate in 3D

Structural, architectural and MEP models are overlaid and clashes are resolved on screen with documented reports.

03

Issue shop drawings

Coordinated drawings for formwork, reinforcement, openings, ceilings, joinery and service routes go to the consultant for approval.

04

Approve materials and mock-ups

Samples, method statements and full-scale mock-ups are signed off before bulk procurement.

05

Build under hold points

Each stage passes inspection before the next starts, with regular progress, quality and safety reports.

06

Test, hand over, support

Commissioning, snagging and as-built drawings, then O&M manuals, warranties and support during the defects period.

ABOUT US

About us

Inspired by precision. Built to last.

MKS Group is an engineering consultancy and general contracting firm based in New Maadi, Cairo. We believe a successful project starts with a clear design and is delivered through precise execution — so design, engineering and construction work as one team with us.

We began our careers delivering residential, commercial, healthcare and industrial projects in Kuwait. We founded MKS Group to bring the same engineering-led, documented way of working to Egypt, with a licensed design office, a site team and a quality team under one roof.

We take projects from concept to completion: architectural and interior design with realistic 3D renders, architectural, structural and MEP working and shop drawings, through to on-site execution, supervision and handover. What you see in the render is what you receive on site.

We work directly with owners and alongside engineering offices on apartments, villas and commercial projects — holding every project to accurate drawings, on-time delivery and transparent costs.
